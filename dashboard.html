03 / From Sun to net energy

One connected engineering model.

Select a stage to reveal how its output feeds the next one. Mechanical and optical frames must agree before an irradiance result can be trusted.

MATLAB / Simulink and Simscape Multibody architecture described in the source documents.
Temporal and geographic input. Day of year, local time, latitude and longitude define the apparent Sun position and drive tracker references.
01 · Solar position

Spencer declination + hour angle

H = (π / 12) · (hsolar − 12)
αs = asin(sinφ sinδ + cosφ cosδ cosH)

Time correction uses longitude, time zone and equation of time. An atan2-based azimuth preserves the correct morning / afternoon quadrant.

02 · Plane-of-array

Beam + sky diffuse + ground-reflected

GPOA = DNI·max(cosθi,0) + DHI·(1+cosβ)/2 + GHI·ρg·(1−cosβ)/2

Incidence depends on calibrated optical tilt. The monofacial base case neglects ground reflection with ρg = 0.

03 · Net balance

Generated energy is not all useful energy

Enet = EPV − Eact
Pm,i = |τi · q̇i|

Ideal joint work is a lower bound: motor, gearbox, driver, friction, wind and auxiliary electronics are not fully represented.

q₀ = 1.6 radMechanical–optical calibration. Joint tilt is not used directly as the optical panel tilt: β(t) = qtilt(t) − q₀. This aligns the multibody frame with the normal vector used in the irradiance model.

Minimum control architecture

A robust astronomical reference; optical sensors can provide fine correction but are not required by the primary tracking scheme.

01
Time + position

DS3231 RTC or optional GPS; date, time and coordinates establish solar position.

02
Control + feedback

Microcontroller, two H-bridge drivers and optional encoders / potentiometers for homing.

03
Motion + safety

Geared actuators, mechanical travel limits and a smooth night return; wind protection is essential.

04
Autonomous power

12 V battery, charge controller and DC–DC regulation for control electronics.
